Ikenna Design System

A minimalist luxury design system built with atomic design principles. Black, white, and gold color palette with elegant serif typography for a sophisticated, high-end aesthetic.

Atomic Design: Components are organized into Atoms (basic elements), Molecules (simple combinations), and Organisms (complex sections) for maximum reusability and maintainability.
